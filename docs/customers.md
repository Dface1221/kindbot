# Target Users & Use Cases

Expert to Skill serves engineers, operations teams, and AI agent developers:

1. **AI Agent Developers:** Convert SOPs and operational playbooks into `SKILL.md` files consumable by autonomous coding and operation agents.
2. **DevOps & Site Reliability Engineers:** Convert incident resolution steps, deployment runbooks, and debugging checklists into standardized skills.
3. **Engineering Teams:** Capture domain knowledge and team procedures into reusable repositories.
